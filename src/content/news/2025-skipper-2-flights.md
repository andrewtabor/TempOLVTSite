Skipper 2, OLVT's two-stage testbed, flew twice in 2025 and reached a maximum altitude of 4,800 ft on a K2050ST booster and a J250FJ sustainer.

The flights tested technologies Hokie 0.75 depends on: active black-powder stage separation, redundant CO₂-powered recovery deployment, dual flight computers and head-end ignition. Read more on the [Skipper 2 page](/vehicles/skipper-2/).
