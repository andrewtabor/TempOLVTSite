OLVT's leadership for 2026–27 is in place. Drew Samtmann and Bella Guereca are co-presidents, serving as chief engineer and program manager, with Luke Thomas as vice president.

Thirteen subteam leads run Avionics, Business, Launch Operations, Propulsion, Research & Development, Structures, and Trajectory & Aerodynamic Analysis. You can see the full roster on the [Team page](/team/).
