Every OLVT member earns a Tripoli Level 1 high-power rocketry certification during onboarding, and The Certifier is the rocket they do it with. Members build their own kit, pass a range safety officer's check before flight, and take full responsibility for how their build performs.

Certifying new members in bulk raises the team's baseline experience and keeps a strong relationship with our local Tripoli New River Valley prefecture. With an added avionics bay, the same scratch-built kit converts for Level 2 certification flights.
