Test Turkey is a subscale version of Hokie 0.75, built to verify key structural, electrical and propulsion design elements in relevant flight conditions.

It flew on a CTI L1720, reaching 5,875 ft, a peak vertical acceleration of 16.0 G and a maximum velocity of 739 ft/s. The flight also showed the team could model its vehicles accurately and analyze the data it brought back. The team planned a relaunch on a CTI N10000 to better match the flight conditions Hokie 0.75 will see.
