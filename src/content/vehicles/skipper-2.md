Skipper 2 is a two-stage rocket, almost entirely manufactured in-house, that served as a testbed for technologies Hokie 0.75 needs. It flew active black-powder stage separation, redundant CO₂-powered recovery deployment, dual flight computers and head-end ignition. Skipper 1 became its upper stage, and the Research & Development subteam built its interstage.

Skipper 2 flew twice in 2025, a major step toward the team's first space shot.
