Skipper 1 is a single-stage, subscale solid-motor test rocket, built entirely in-house from composite materials with a dual recovery deployment system. It proved out design concepts the team will scale up for Hokie 0.75, and it went on to serve as the upper stage of Skipper 2.

OLVT flew five variations of Skipper 1, ending with Skipper 1E in April 2024.
