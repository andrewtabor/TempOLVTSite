Hokie 0.75 is OLVT's first space shot: a two-stage sounding rocket designed to carry a 5 kg payload past the Kármán line, the 100 km boundary of space. It is also the step between the team's test vehicles and Hokie 1, the orbital launch vehicle OLVT is working toward.

Like every OLVT vehicle, it is being designed with a systems engineering approach. The airframe is mostly aluminum, with a fiberglass sustainer electronics bay and nose cone to save weight and stay transparent to radio. The booster lands under a single parachute, and the sustainer uses dual deployment to limit wind drift. A custom avionics system combines off-the-shelf flight hardware with radio hardware the team built itself.

Test Turkey, Skipper 1 and Skipper 2 each flew pieces of its design first.
